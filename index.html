Welcome

Good for nature, fair for people

We run jungle treks and village tours that keep the impact on the rainforest low and the benefit for the local community high. Our guides are local, speak English, and follow the national park rules and our own jungle code.

From a short family walk to a five-day expedition, every trip is led by people who grew up at the edge of this forest. We hire locally, pay fair wages and support village initiatives.
